DawaTrack Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how DawaTrack ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, and protects your personal information when you use our medicine reminder application.

Information We Collect

  • Personal Information: Name, email address, age, gender, and contact information provided during account creation and profile setup.
  • Medical Information: Medicine names, prescription details, dosage information, medical appointment schedules, stock levels, and expiry dates.
  • Usage Data: Information about how you use the application, including reminder settings, preferences, analytics data about medicine adherence patterns, and medicine reorder history.
  • Calendar Data: With your permission, we access your Google Calendar to create medicine and appointment reminders.
  • Images: Prescription images that you upload to the application.
  • Analytics Data: Statistical data about medicine consumption patterns, reminder effectiveness, and health tracking metrics.
  • Search History: Your medicine search queries and interactions with the medicine library.

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain our medicine reminder service
  • To send reminder notifications about medicines and appointments
  • To create and manage calendar events for your reminders
  • To generate analytics and statistics about your medicine adherence
  • To store and manage your prescription images securely
  • To track medicine stock levels and send reorder notifications
  • To provide personalized health tips and medical information
  • To maintain your medicine library and search history
  • To improve our application and user experience
  • To communicate with you about your account and updates

Data Storage and Security

We use Supabase for secure data storage and management. All data is encrypted both in transit and at rest using industry-standard encryption protocols. We implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures to protect against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction of your information.

Image Storage and Processing

When you upload prescription images to DawaTrack:

  • Images are stored securely in our encrypted database
  • Images are only accessible to you through your authenticated account
  • You can delete your uploaded images at any time
  • We do not perform any automated analysis or processing on your prescription images

Analytics and Statistics

We generate analytics and statistics about medicine adherence and health tracking to:

  • Help you understand your medicine consumption patterns
  • Provide insights about your adherence to prescribed schedules
  • Generate visual representations of your health data
  • All analytics are performed locally and stored securely in your account

Health Tips and Medical Information

Our application provides health tips and medical information:

  • All health-related content is for informational purposes only
  • Content is regularly updated but should not replace professional medical advice
  • Your interaction with health tips and articles may be tracked to improve content relevance
  • Medical news and updates are sourced from reputable healthcare sources

Stock Management and Notifications

Our stock management features include:

  • Tracking medicine quantities and expiry dates
  • Automated notifications for low stock levels
  • Expiry date reminders and alerts
  • Reorder history tracking

Sensor Dashboard and Health Monitoring

Our sensor dashboard provides real-time health monitoring:

  • Health Metrics: We collect and process real-time health data including heart rate, blood pressure, and other vital signs from connected sensors.
  • Data Visualization: Health metrics are displayed through interactive charts and gauges for easy monitoring.
  • Alert System: Automated alerts are generated when health metrics exceed normal ranges.
  • Data Storage: Health sensor data is stored securely and encrypted in our database.
  • Data Retention: Sensor data is retained for a limited period to provide historical analysis and trend monitoring.
  • Third-Party Sensors: We support integration with various health monitoring devices and sensors.

DawaDost Chatbot

Our AI-powered chatbot (DawaDost) provides health assistance:

  • Conversation Data: We collect and process your conversations with DawaDost to improve responses and service quality.
  • AI Processing: Conversations are processed using Google's Gemini AI model to provide accurate health-related information.
  • Data Usage: Chat history is used to maintain conversation context and provide personalized responses.
  • Privacy Measures: All conversations are encrypted and stored securely.
  • Data Sharing: Chat data is not shared with third parties except for necessary AI processing through Google's Gemini API.
  • User Control: You can clear your chat history at any time through the application settings.

Data Retention

We retain your personal information only for as long as necessary to provide you with our services and for legitimate business purposes. You can request deletion of your account and associated data at any time through the application settings.

Google Calendar Integration

When you use our Google Calendar integration:

  • We only request access to create and manage events related to your medicine reminders
  • We don't access or read your existing calendar events
  • You can revoke our calendar access at any time through your Google Account settings

Data Sharing

We do not sell or share your personal information with third parties except as necessary to provide our services (such as Google Calendar integration) or as required by law.

Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Supabase: For secure database management and authentication
  • Google Calendar API: For creating and managing reminder events
  • Chart.js: For generating analytics visualizations

Each third-party service has its own privacy policy and data handling practices. We recommend reviewing their respective privacy policies.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal information
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your information
  • Withdraw consent for data processing

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or your personal information, please contact us at dawatrack.official@gmail.com

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date.
