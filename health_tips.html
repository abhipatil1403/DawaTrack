Medicine Safety Tips

Essential guidelines for storing, organizing, and managing your medications safely.

  • Temperature Control
    Store medicines between 15-25°C (59-77°F), away from heat, light, and humidity
  • Proper Storage
    Keep original packaging with labels and information leaflets intact. Store in a dedicated, lockable cabinet
  • Organization
    Use pill organizers with alarms, maintain an inventory list, and check expiration dates monthly
  • Cross-contamination
    Keep different medications separate, never mix in same container. Use clean, dry hands when handling
  • Travel Safety
    Carry medicines in hand luggage, keep a list of prescriptions, and bring extra doses for emergencies
  • Emergency Preparedness
    Keep emergency contacts updated, including pharmacy and healthcare provider numbers

Diet & Nutrition Tips

Important dietary considerations to maximize medication effectiveness and maintain overall health.

  • Medication Timing
    Some medications work best on empty stomach, others with food. Check with your healthcare provider
  • Hydration Impact
    Drink 8 glasses of water daily. Proper hydration helps medication absorption and reduces side effects
  • Food Interactions
    Avoid grapefruit, high-calcium foods, and caffeine with certain medications. Check specific interactions
  • Nutrient Balance
    Include protein, fiber, and vitamins in your diet. Some medications may affect nutrient absorption
  • Supplement Coordination
    Space supplements 2-4 hours from medications. Consult healthcare provider about interactions
  • Alcohol Awareness
    Many medications interact with alcohol. Always check labels and consult healthcare provider
